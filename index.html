A software company rooted in Eldoret

TriCore Solutions is a software, website and application development company based in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County. For over two years we have worked with local businesses and organizations that need reliable software without paying for features they will never use.

Every project starts with a conversation about the problem, not a sales pitch. We then build in small, working steps so you see progress early and can change direction before it costs you time or money.

Our Mission

To design and build software, websites and applications that are affordable, reliable and genuinely useful for businesses in Eldoret and across Kenya.

Our Vision

To become a trusted technology partner for growing Kenyan businesses, known for clear communication and software that keeps working long after launch.

Why work with TriCore

  • Software, websites and apps built specifically for your business, not a generic template
  • Direct communication throughout the project, with no middlemen
  • Based locally in Eldoret and familiar with the Kenyan market
  • Fair, transparent pricing agreed before work begins
  • Support after launch, not just a handover and goodbye
